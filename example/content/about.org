#+title: About

* About this site

denden.el is a static-site library built on Org's own =ox-publish=. A
site layer supplies its own content, theme, and page-dispatch function;
this folder is the smallest one that still works.

Two kinds of page live here. This one and the home page are plain: a
title and some prose. Anything under [[/blog/][blog/]] gets a date above
its content and shows up in the post list.

Read =example-site.el= and =example-theme.el= for the whole thing.
