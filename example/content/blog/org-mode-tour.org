#+title: An Org-Mode Tour
#+date: [2026-03-02 Mon]

A longer post, mostly as a tour of what plain Org gives you for free once
it goes through the =denden-html= backend: *bold*, /italic/, ~code~,
=verbatim=, and +strikethrough+, all native syntax, no shortcodes.
