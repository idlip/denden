#org #writing

Org has more than paragraphs and lists. Here is how a few of its other pieces come out of the default layout.

Callouts#

Note

A note block becomes a labelled callout. The site decides which block types are callouts.

Tip

Write the block in Org as #+begin_tip and #+end_tip.

Warning

Callouts are for asides. A page made of them is hard to read.
